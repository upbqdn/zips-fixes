# Terminology

The key word "SHOULD" in this document is to be interpreted as described in
BCP 14 [^BCP14] when, and only when, it appears in all capitals.

Fee terminology is as defined in ZIP 317 [^zip-0317]; "zatoshi" is as defined in
the Zcash protocol specification. [^protocol]

# Abstract

This ZIP cuts ZIP 317's [^zip-0317] `marginal_fee` from 5,000 to 1,000
zatoshis per logical action, so the minimum conventional fee drops from
10,000 to 2,000 zatoshis. It also raises `weight_ratio_cap` in ZIP 317's
block template algorithm from 4 to 10, so that the fee cut does not narrow
the range as sharply, and updates the parts of ZIP 317's rationale that
depend on these values. Wallets switch to the new fee together at an
activation height, so that the fee they pay does not reveal whether their
software has been updated.

# Motivation

ZIP 317's fee was set in late 2022, when ZEC was about $30 and the minimum
fee of 10,000 zatoshis cost about a third of a cent in USD. The fee is fixed
in zatoshis, so when the price went up, so did the fee. At $800 (the price on
2026-08-29) the minimum fee costs 8 cents.

Cutting `marginal_fee` to 1,000 brings the minimum fee to 2,000 zatoshis,
about 1.6 cents. Even after a 5x cut, it is still over 5x what it was when
ZEC was $30. It only gets cheaper than the 2022 fee if ZEC falls below $150.

Additionally, the fee at which a transaction's weight saturates is
`weight_ratio_cap` times its conventional fee, so cutting `marginal_fee`
shrinks the range of inclusion-influencing fees from
10,000 - 40,000 zatoshis to 2,000 - 8,000. Raising `weight_ratio_cap` to 10
widens that range to 2,000 - 20,000, still below today's maximum. It also
keeps transactions from wallets that have not yet updated, which pay 5x the
new conventional fee, from being clamped at the cap.

# Privacy Implications

*Privacy:* If wallets switch to the new fee at different times, the fee
reveals which wallet software made the transaction. The Deployment section
therefore has wallets switch at a common activation height rather than when
each release ships.

# Specification

## Changes to ZIP 317

In the parameter table of the **Fee calculation** section of ZIP 317
[^zip-0317], the row

```rst
:math:`\mathit{marginal\_fee}`                 :math:`5000`  zatoshis per logical action (as defined below)
```

is replaced with

```rst
:math:`\mathit{marginal\_fee}`                 :math:`1000`  zatoshis per logical action (as defined below)
```

No other entry in the table changes.

If ZIP 235 [^zip-0235] activates, the fraction removed from circulation is
unchanged: of a 2,000-zatoshi fee, 1,200 zatoshis are removed and 800 go to
the miner.

In the section **Rationale for the chosen parameters** of ZIP 317, the
paragraph under **Marginal Fee** is replaced with

> With `marginal_fee` = 1000, the conventional fee for a minimal transaction (as
> described above) is 2,000 zatoshis. ZIP 2009 gives the rationale for this
> value, which reduces the fee cost of potential denial-of-service attacks by a
> factor of 5 relative to the original value of 5,000 zatoshis.

The maximum additional fee for a ZIP 231 memo bundle given under **Memo
Chunks** in the same section is proportional to `marginal_fee`; its amounts in
ZEC are divided by 5.

## Change to `weight_ratio_cap`

In the section **Recommended algorithm for block template construction** of
ZIP 317, replace the constant definition `weight_ratio_cap` = 4 with
`weight_ratio_cap` = 10, and update the rationale as follows:

> The rationale for choosing `weight_ratio_cap` = 10 is as a compromise between
> not allowing any prioritization of transactions relative to those that pay the
> conventional fee, and allowing arbitrary prioritization based on ability to
> pay. The fee at which a transaction's weight ratio saturates is
> `weight_ratio_cap` times its conventional fee, so it falls with `marginal_fee`.
> A cap of 10 at `marginal_fee` = 1000 places that point below where it was at
> `weight_ratio_cap` = 4 and `marginal_fee` = 5000. It also raises the limit on
> the relative probability of picking a transaction, compared with one that pays
> exactly the conventional fee, from 4 to 10.

## Interaction with the `getstandardfee` RPC endpoint

This section is non-normative.

The `getstandardfee` RPC endpoint of zebrad reports the fee per logical action
that wallets should pay for a transaction in the next block. Endpoints that report
ZIP 317's `marginal_fee` in this way are expected to follow the Deployment
section: 5,000 zatoshis per logical action while the next block is below the
Mainnet activation height, and 1,000 zatoshis from that height and on Testnet.

# Rationale

**Why a `marginal_fee` of 1,000.** A 5x cut still leaves the fee over 5x above its 2022
dollar cost as of 2026-08-29.

**Why a `weight_ratio_cap` of 10.** In ZIP 317, section "Rationale for block template
construction algorithm", the cap limits the relative probability of picking a transaction
to at most `weight_ratio_cap` times that of a transaction paying exactly the conventional
fee. Raising the cap from 4 to 10 relaxes that bound 2.5-fold: a transaction can obtain
up to 10 times the selection weight of one paying the conventional fee (4 times before),
at a fee of at most 10 times the conventional fee. This is accepted for two reasons:
the fee at which the weight saturates for a minimal transaction, 20,000 zatoshis, stays
below the 40,000 zatoshis of the previous parameters; and transactions from wallets still
paying 5,000 zatoshis per logical action, 5 times the new conventional fee, keep their
full weight during the transition instead of being clamped at a cap of 4.

**Precedent.** ZIP 313 [^zip-0313] reduced the conventional fee in 2020 the
same way: a wallet convention, no network upgrade.

# Security Considerations

The **Denial of Service** section of ZIP 317 names two protections against
transaction-rate-based denial of service: the block size limit and transaction
fees. Reducing `marginal_fee` by a factor of 5 reduces by the same factor the fee
cost of filling blocks with transactions that pay the conventional fee. The block
size limit is unchanged.

# Deployment

Nodes need to run a release implementing this ZIP before wallets adopt the new
fee. `block_unpaid_action_limit` is 0 in both zebra and zakura and is not
operator-configurable, so an un-upgraded node will not relay a 2,000-zatoshi
transaction at all, since it counts 2 unpaid actions under the old parameters.

The activation height is Mainnet block height 3590000. Wallets SHOULD use
`marginal_fee = 1000` for transactions whose target height (the height of the
next block when the transaction is created) is at least the activation height,
and 5000 below it. On Testnet, wallets SHOULD use `marginal_fee = 1000` as soon
as they implement this ZIP.

At a 75-second target block spacing throughout, the activation height would be
reached in mid December 2026. NU7 reduces the target spacing to 25 seconds from
its Mainnet activation height NU7_MAINNET_ACTIVATION_HEIGHT [^zip-0218]
[^zip-0259], so each block at a height from NU7_MAINNET_ACTIVATION_HEIGHT to
3589999 inclusive brings that date forward by 50 seconds.
The activation height is past the end-of-service halt of
both zebra v6.3.0 (height 3564960) and zakura v1.3.1 (height 3501339), by
which point nodes running those releases have halted.

The `weight_ratio_cap` change affects block producers only and has no wallet
dependency, so the two parameter changes can be adopted in either order.

# Reference implementation

Zebra implements both parameter changes from v6.4.0 [^zebra-11290], and reports
the activation schedule through `getstandardfee` [^zebra-11557].
